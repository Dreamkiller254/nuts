# Nuts Paradise design collection

Open [the gallery](https://nuts.webunbounded.com/index.html) to compare all six responsive homepage concepts.

The numbered floating navigation switches between concepts. Navigation links scroll to the relevant homepage sections; mobile menus, product enquiry controls and the concept 5 process accordions are interactive.

Enquiry forms are previews. They validate the entered information and download a text brief locally. They do not submit, transmit or persist buyer details. A production contact destination and backend were not supplied.

Photography is real illustrative stock photography, not evidence of Nuts Paradise facilities, employees, growers, product specifications or freight arrangements. Original source links and photographer credits are on `/photography.html`. Product categories, location, capacity and certification copy follow the supplied `design.md`; no additional product grades, contacts, certifications or destination markets are invented.

All photographs, fonts, CSS and JavaScript are served locally. Font licenses accompany the font files. No package installation or build step is required. Original layouts remain in the first two HTML files, with their visual refinements in `assets/refresh.css`. New concepts have individual stylesheets and share accessible navigation, enquiry dialogs and reduced-motion support through `assets/common.css` and `assets/common.js`.

Desktop thumbnail captures for the gallery are in `assets/previews/`. The browser review covers desktop and mobile rendering, image loading, section links, mobile menus, enquiry downloads, product preselection, the design switcher and layout widths from 320 to 1440 pixels.

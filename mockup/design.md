# Nuts Paradise — Website Design Brief

## Project goal
Rebuild Nuts Paradise as the website of a credible South African **macadamia and cashew processor and exporter**, not a broker and not a consumer snack shop.

Primary positioning:

**Nuts Paradise**  
**South African Macadamia & Cashew Processor & Exporter**  
**African Nuts. Processed for Global Markets.**

The website must feel credible to professional international buyers: importers, distributors, food manufacturers, retailers/private-label buyers and ingredient buyers.

## Verified business facts
Use these facts consistently and do not embellish them:
- Processing facility: Riverside Park Industrial Zone, Rapid Street, Riverside Park, Mbombela, South Africa
- Processing capacity: up to 1,000 MT per month
- Certification: FSSC 22000
- FSSC 22000 applies to both macadamia and cashew processing
- Administrative office: Smit Street, Braamfontein 2000, Johannesburg, South Africa
- Product categories: Macadamias and Cashews
- Business model: South African processor and exporter

## Primary conversion goal
The main goal is a completed buyer enquiry / quote request.

Primary CTA: **Request a Quote**

Supporting CTAs:
- Explore Our Products
- Discuss Your Product Requirements
- Request Product Information
- Speak to Our Export Team
- Contact Nuts Paradise

Every major page should end with a clear enquiry CTA.

## Audience
Design for B2B buyers, procurement teams and food-industry decision makers. The site should answer these questions quickly:
1. What does Nuts Paradise process?
2. Where is processing done?
3. What level of quality assurance exists?
4. What is the processing capacity?
5. Can they support international supply requirements?
6. How do I start a product/quote discussion?

## Design personality
Keywords:
- premium
- clean
- modern
- confident
- operational
- agricultural origin + industrial processing
- precise
- trustworthy
- export focused
- spacious

Avoid the visual language of a retail snack store, lifestyle food blog or commodity broker.

## Suggested visual system
### Colour direction
Use a restrained palette built around:
- deep forest green
- warm macadamia / cashew / earth tones
- off-white / warm cream
- charcoal
- white

Avoid neon greens, overly rustic brown palettes and excessive gold luxury styling.

### Typography
Use strong typography with obvious hierarchy. A refined editorial serif may be paired with a neutral modern sans-serif, or use a premium grotesk throughout. Headings should feel confident, not decorative.

### Layout
- generous whitespace
- strong grid
- large image areas
- clear section rhythm
- restrained borders and dividers
- short, scannable proof points
- bold CTA placement
- responsive mobile-first behavior

## Photography direction
Priority order:
1. Authentic Nuts Paradise facility photography from Mbombela
2. Macadamia and cashew close-ups
3. Processing activity
4. Quality-control activity
5. Packed finished product / export-ready operational imagery
6. South African landscape imagery used sparingly

Images should feel premium and operational rather than grocery-store focused.

Do not use:
- unrelated factory imagery presented as Nuts Paradise
- other companies' facilities
- packaging that implies products Nuts Paradise has not approved
- fake certification badges
- machinery or production steps that are not actually used

When approved photography is not available in a mockup, use clearly labelled image placeholders rather than pretending stock photography is the real facility.

## Homepage information architecture
### 1. Header
Navigation:
- Home
- About Us
- Products
- Processing
- Quality & Certification
- Traceability
- Buyers
- Export Markets
- Contact Us

Primary header CTA: **Request a Quote**

Desktop may use a compact mega/dropdown approach if needed to keep the navigation elegant. Mobile should use a clean menu drawer.

### 2. Hero
Heading:
**South African Macadamia & Cashew Processing for Global Markets**

Supporting message should establish Nuts Paradise as a South African processor/exporter serving international importers, distributors, manufacturers, retailers and ingredient buyers.

Hero CTAs:
- Request a Quote
- Explore Our Products

Trust line:
**FSSC 22000 Certified Processing | 1,000 MT Monthly Capacity | South Africa**

Hero visual should ideally show approved macadamia/cashew processing imagery.

### 3. Introduction
Heading:
**From African Origin to Global Market**

Explain that professional buyers need processing, quality management, product preparation, packing and dependable export-focused execution — not merely access to nuts.

### 4. Why Nuts Paradise
Four proof points:
- South African Processing
- FSSC 22000 Certified
- 1,000 MT Monthly Capacity
- Export-Focused Supply

These should look like evidence, not decorative marketing cards.

### 5. Product categories
Two strong category features only:
- Macadamias
- Cashews

Do not add pecans, almonds or pistachios to the rebuild unless the approved business brief changes.

### 6. Processing
Heading:
**Processing in Mbombela, South Africa**

Show the Riverside Park processing location and explain that processing, quality controls and export readiness are handled as a connected operation.

### 7. Quality
Heading:
**Quality You Can Build Into Your Supply Chain**

Communicate FSSC 22000 certification for both product categories without inventing additional certifications or endorsements.

### 8. Buyer / quote CTA
Heading:
**Looking for a South African Macadamia or Cashew Processing Partner?**

Strong Request a Quote CTA.

Design a coherent system for:

4. Processing

The home page should be the richest design; internal pages should inherit the same typography, navigation, visual language, buttons, forms and spacing system.

## Product page constraints
Do not invent or publish unverified:
- grades
- kernel styles
- sizes
- product codes
- packaging types
- shelf-life data
- nutrition panels

The product design should make it easy to add approved specification sheets later.

## Traceability constraints
Do not claim:
- farm-level traceability
- blockchain traceability
- full origin traceability
- real-time portal tracking
- batch-level documentation
unless Nuts Paradise verifies and approves those capabilities.

## Export constraints
Do not invent:
- destination countries
- shipping ports
- Incoterms
- freight partners
- customs capabilities
- market approvals

## Contact constraints
Until approved contact details are supplied, do not make a phone number or email address part of the final design. Do not display exact map pins until their presentation is approved.

Quote form fields:
- Full name
- Company name
- Business email address
- Country
- Product interest: Macadamias / Cashews / Both
- Estimated monthly or annual volume
- Intended destination market
- Message
- Consent checkbox

Submit: **Send Enquiry**

## Concept directions to explore
### Concept A — Origin to Export
Editorial, warm and premium. Forest-green hero, cream backgrounds, natural nut tones, serif-led headlines, large approved product/facility imagery, clear story from African origin to South African processing to global buyer.

### Concept B — Processing Confidence
More architectural and industrial. White/charcoal base, dark green accents, strong grid, oversized capacity/certification data, facility-oriented imagery, precise diagrams/process flows and procurement-friendly information hierarchy.

### Concept C — Modern African Export House
A third AI-generated option may explore a more distinctive African identity through restrained geometry, texture and landscape colour — without becoming decorative, tourist-oriented or consumer-snack branding.

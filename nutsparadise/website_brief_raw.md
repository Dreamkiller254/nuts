14. Design and photography direction
The website should look like a credible B2B food-processing and export business.

Style
Premium, clean and modern

Agricultural origin combined with industrial processing confidence

Minimal, spacious layouts

Strong typography and clear calls to action

Use deep green, warm natural nut tones, charcoal and white

Avoid overly decorative or consumer-snack branding

Image priorities
Authentic facility images from Mbombela

Close-up macadamia and cashew product photography

Processing and quality-control activity

Packed products and export-ready operational imagery

South African landscape imagery used sparingly

Avoid
Generic images of unrelated factories

Images that imply unsupported packaging or retail products

Stock imagery showing other companies’ facilities

Consumer-focused product claims

Decorative badges for unverified certifications

15. Technical and content implementation requirements
Make the website fully responsive for mobile, tablet and desktop.

Build all forms with spam protection and email routing to an approved Nuts Paradise inbox.

Add clear form validation and an enquiry-success confirmation message.

Use compressed, high-resolution images and modern image formats for fast loading.

Add alt text to every meaningful image.

Add Google Search Console and analytics only once Nuts Paradise supplies the relevant access details.

Set up editable website content so approved staff can update copy, images and product information.

Create a privacy policy and cookie notice appropriate to the website’s enquiry form and analytics configuration.

Add schema markup for the business only after confirming the approved business name, website URL, contact details and locations.

Do not publish unverified operational claims or add content merely to make pages appear more complete.
